## Two opposite reward models

**Bybit Card** pays cashback by **spending tier**, from 2% up to 10%, in MNT (its native token) — crucially **without staking**. You spend, you earn; nothing is locked. The card is free (€0/year), on the Mastercard network.

Both cards reward in a volatile in-house token, so that's a wash. The decisive difference is **staking**. Bybit gives you a meaningful cashback (2%+) the moment you spend, with nothing locked. Crypto.com's headline 5% is a **conditional ceiling**: no CRO staked means roughly 0% back.
