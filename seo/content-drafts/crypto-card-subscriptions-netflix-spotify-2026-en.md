## "Normal" cashback vs subscription rebate

Two different things:

- **Classic cashback**: any crypto cashback card pays its usual % on a Netflix or Spotify payment, like any other purchase.
- **Subscription rebate**: some cards refund **all or part of the subscription** (Netflix, Spotify, Amazon Prime, Disney+…), effectively making it free or nearly so. That's where the real value is.

## How to actually benefit

**Plutus** is the most subscription-focused: its Perks refund one or more subscriptions each month, depending on your plan and PLU staking. If you stack several subscriptions, the rebates can exceed the card's ~€98/year — run the numbers on your real subscriptions.

**Crypto.com** rebates Netflix, Spotify and Amazon Prime, but **only on its upper tiers**, which require staking CRO. Without high staking you don't get it — just the base cashback.

For every other card, a subscription payment is a purchase like any other: you earn the card's usual cashback, with no dedicated rebate.

## So which should you choose?

- **You have several subscriptions and want to make them pay** → **Plutus**, whose Perks are built for this (if you cover the fee).
- **You're already on Crypto.com** at a high tier → Netflix/Spotify/Prime rebates are included.
- **You just want simple cashback** on your spend (subscriptions included) → go for a good [cashback card](/en/crypto-card-cashback) with no staking.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which crypto card rebates Netflix and Spotify?**
Plutus (via its Perks) and Crypto.com (on higher tiers with CRO staking) rebate these subscriptions. Other cards only pay their usual cashback.

**Is the rebate automatic?**
It depends on the plan and conditions (staking, number of active perks, caps). Check the current terms before relying on it.

**Is it really worth it?**
If you stack several subscriptions, the rebates can exceed the card's cost. With a single subscription the upside is smaller — do the math.

**Can a free card rebate subscriptions?**
Rarely in full: subscription rebates are mostly tied to paid/staked plans (Plutus, Crypto.com tiers). A free card tends to pay simple cashback instead.
