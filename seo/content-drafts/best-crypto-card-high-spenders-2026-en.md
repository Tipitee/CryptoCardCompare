## Why the "biggest %" isn't always the best

At high volume, three things erode the real return:

- **Caps**: many cards cap monthly cashback. Above the cap you earn 0% — the headline rate becomes theoretical.
- **Lock-up cost**: a high rate that requires locking a token (CRO, PLU) ties up capital. That capital has an opportunity cost, and the token can fall.
- **Fee amortisation**: a card with an annual fee (e.g. Plutus) only pays off once your spend is high enough to cover it.

For a high spender, the real sum is: **net cashback = (real rate under the cap) − lock-up cost − annual fee**.
