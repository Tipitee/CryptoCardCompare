## What actually matters when you live abroad

For someone moving between countries or living outside their home country, the criteria differ from a simple trip:

- **FX fees**: you pay in several currencies all year. A 2–3% spread on every purchase adds up. Aim for **0% FX**.
- **Worldwide acceptance**: Visa/Mastercard everywhere, no geographic blocking.
- **IBAN**: to receive a salary, invoice, or top up the card from a local account.
- **Spend without fully converting**: keep your crypto exposure while paying for daily life.

This is a distinct need from a simple [travel card](/en/crypto-card-travel): here it's a **permanent**, multi-currency use, not a few days of holiday.

## How to choose by profile

**You move a lot and pay in several currencies** → prioritise **0% FX**: Nexo and Gnosis Pay avoid the spread on every purchase. Over a year of multi-currency spending, that often saves more than any cashback.

**You receive a salary or invoice from abroad** → you need an **IBAN**: a regulated card like Deblock or an account-backed card like Bitpanda makes incoming transfers simpler.

**You want to keep your crypto exposure** → Nexo lets you spend via a credit line without liquidating your portfolio — handy when your income is in crypto.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the best crypto card for a digital nomad?**
One that combines 0% FX and worldwide acceptance: Nexo or Gnosis Pay. Add an IBAN (Deblock, Bitpanda) if you receive transfers.

**Do FX fees really matter?**
Yes. Abroad, you constantly pay in other currencies; a 2–3% spread per purchase weighs more than cashback over a full year.

**Can you receive a salary on a crypto card?**
Via a card backed by an IBAN account (Deblock, Bitpanda), you can receive transfers then spend. Check the conditions and your residence's tax rules.

**Does a crypto card work in every country?**
Visa/Mastercard cards are widely accepted, but sign-up availability depends on your country of residence. Check the card's page before applying.
