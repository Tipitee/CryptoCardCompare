Some even provide an **IBAN** to receive transfers. For the unbanked or underbanked, it's a real alternative. Data checked 2026.

## Why a crypto card can replace (part of) a bank account

A crypto card doesn't depend on a traditional bank:

- **No credit check**: access doesn't depend on a banking history or a credit score.
- **Prepaid or wallet model**: you spend what you load (crypto/stablecoins), not an overdraft.
- **Optional IBAN**: some regulated cards provide an IBAN, so you can receive a salary or transfers like a current account.
- **Fast onboarding**: often minutes via an app, with a simple identity check (KYC).

Note: most regulated issuers require an **identity check (KYC)** — "no bank account" doesn't mean "no identity". For anonymity, see our dedicated no-KYC page instead.

## How it works in practice

You download the app, complete an identity check, and get a card (often virtual first, addable to Apple/Google Pay). You fund it with crypto or stablecoins and pay like with a regular card — the merchant receives fiat. If the card offers an **IBAN**, you can also receive transfers and use it as a backup main account.

To limit volatility, fund in **stablecoins** (USDC, EURe): you spend a stable value, close to the pound or euro.

## So which should you choose?

- **You want an IBAN to receive transfers** → Deblock or Bitpanda (IBAN by country).
- **You want to keep control of your funds** (self-custody) → Gnosis Pay or Deblock.
- **You want to spend without selling your crypto** → Nexo (credit line).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can you get a card without a bank account?**
Yes. Prepaid or wallet-backed crypto cards need no bank account and no credit check; you fund them with crypto or stablecoins.

**Is an identity check required?**
Usually yes (KYC) with regulated issuers. "No bank account" doesn't mean "no identity".

**Can you receive a salary?**
If the card provides an IBAN (Deblock, Bitpanda), you can receive transfers like a current account.

**Is there an overdraft?**
No on prepaid cards: you spend what you load. Nexo offers a credit line backed by your crypto, which is different from a bank overdraft.
