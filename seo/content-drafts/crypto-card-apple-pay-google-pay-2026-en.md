## Why Apple Pay / Google Pay matters

Many crypto cards are issued first as a **virtual card**: without adding them to a mobile wallet, they'd be hard to use in store. Apple Pay and Google Pay bring three things:

- **Contactless payments** with your phone or watch, even without a physical card.
- **Security**: the card number isn't shared with the merchant (tokenisation).
- **Instant use**: you can spend as soon as the virtual card is issued, without waiting for plastic.

## How to set it up

In the issuer's app, choose "Add to Apple Wallet" or "Add to Google Wallet", or add the card manually in your phone's Wallet app. The virtual card is often usable **immediately**. A few things to watch:

- **Country availability**: some issuers roll out Apple/Google Pay market by market.
- **Contactless limits**: above a certain amount, a PIN may be requested depending on the bank/merchant.
- **Virtual vs physical card**: if you want to withdraw cash, you'll also need a physical card (see our ATM withdrawal guide).

## Frequently asked questions

**Which crypto cards work with Apple Pay?**
The main ones: Crypto.com, Nexo, Bybit, Bitpanda, Gnosis Pay, MetaMask, Revolut. Check availability in your country.

**What about Google Pay?**
The same major cards generally support Google Pay too. Support can vary by market.

**Can you pay without a physical card?**
Yes: a virtual card added to Apple/Google Pay lets you pay contactless, with no plastic. For cash withdrawals, a physical card is still needed.

**Is it secure?**
Yes. Apple/Google Pay use tokenisation: the real card number isn't shared with the merchant.
