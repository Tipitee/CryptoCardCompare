## Same philosophy: self-custody

Unlike exchange cards (Crypto.com, Nexo, Bybit…), neither card **takes custody** of your funds. You keep your keys; the card debits your wallet at payment, converted to the merchant's currency. That's the core appeal for anyone who wants to stay **on-chain** and non-custodial.

**Gnosis Pay** runs on a **Safe smart account** on Gnosis Chain, with EURe (a euro stablecoin) as its native currency, on the **Visa** network. **MetaMask Card** spends straight from your **MetaMask wallet** (USDC/USDT on Linea), on the **Mastercard** network, via Baanx infrastructure.

## The real trade-off

Both win on the same point — you stay in control of your crypto. The difference is practical:

**MetaMask Card** wins on **simplicity**. If MetaMask is already your wallet, there's almost nothing to set up: you spend what's there, no staking, 1% cashback (up to 3%). It's the most natural entry into an on-chain card for an Ethereum user.

**Gnosis Pay** aims higher for **European** use: EURe is a euro-denominated stablecoin, which cuts everyday FX friction, and cashback climbs to **5%** (with optional tiers). The trade-off is creating a dedicated **Safe account** — one more step than plugging in an existing wallet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Are Gnosis Pay and MetaMask really self-custody?**
Yes. Both debit your own wallet at payment; you keep your keys, unlike an exchange card that holds your funds.

**Which has the better cashback?**
Gnosis Pay goes higher (up to 5% vs 3% for MetaMask), with optional tiers. MetaMask stays simpler, with no staking.

**What currency do you spend?**
Gnosis Pay uses EURe (euro stablecoin) and USDC; MetaMask Card spends USDC/USDT (and wETH) from your wallet.

**Visa or Mastercard?**
Gnosis Pay is on Visa, MetaMask Card on Mastercard. Both are widely accepted across Europe.

**Do I need to stake for cashback?**
With MetaMask, no. With Gnosis Pay, staking is optional and unlocks the higher cashback tiers.
