Short answer: if you hold **stablecoins** (USDC, USDT, EURe), a crypto card lets you spend them like a bank card — with two concrete benefits: **no volatility** at the point of sale, and a near-zero capital gain (so near-zero tax). For a euro stablecoin, **Gnosis Pay** (native EURe) is the obvious pick; for USDC, **MetaMask** or **Nexo** do the job. Data checked 2026.

## Why a "stablecoin" card makes sense

Spending BTC or ETH with a card means converting a volatile asset on every purchase — with the risk of selling at the wrong moment and a gain to calculate. With a **stablecoin**, the value doesn't move: 1 USDC ≈ $1, 1 EURe ≈ €1. The result:

- **No crypto FX surprise**: you spend exactly what you have.
- **Minimal tax**: the gain between receiving and spending is close to zero (see our [cashback tax guide](/en/blog/crypto-card-cashback-tax-uk-2026)).
- **Budget-friendly**: stablecoins behave like on-chain pounds/euros/dollars.

## The euro-stablecoin advantage (EURe)

If you spend mostly in euros, a **euro stablecoin** like EURe avoids any currency conversion: you pay in on-chain euros, the merchant receives euros, with no FX spread. That's the strength of **Gnosis Pay**, built around EURe and self-custody. With USDC (a dollar coin), a small EUR/USD move can remain when paying in the eurozone — worth keeping in mind. UK spenders face a GBP conversion either way, so prioritise low FX fees.

## So which should you choose?

- **You spend in euros and want the least friction** → **Gnosis Pay** (native EURe, self-custody).
- **You already use MetaMask** and hold USDC → **MetaMask Card**, simple, from your wallet.
- **You want to keep your stablecoins as collateral** and spend without moving them → **Nexo** (credit line).

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the best card for spending stablecoins?**
For euros, Gnosis Pay (native EURe, self-custody). For USDC, MetaMask Card or Nexo. All avoid the volatility of a regular token.

**Is spending stablecoins taxable?**
Technically, spending is still a disposal; but a stablecoin's gain is near zero, so the tax is too. See our tax guide.

**EURe or USDC for a euro budget?**
EURe avoids any currency conversion if you spend in euros. USDC can carry a small EUR/USD move at payment.

**Can you earn cashback spending stablecoins?**
Yes, depending on the card. Cashback applies to spending; check the reward currency and caps on each card's page.
